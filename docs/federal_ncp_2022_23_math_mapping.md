# Federal NCP 2022–23 Mathematics Curriculum Mapping (Grades 1–10)
## MathQuest Platform Alignment & Progression Grid

**Curriculum Standard:** National Curriculum of Pakistan (NCP) 2022–23 — Mathematics (Federal Curriculum)  
**Target Scope:** Grades 1 through 10  
**Core Strands Covered:**
1. Numbers and Operations
2. Algebra
3. Geometry and Measurement
4. Statistics and Probability
5. Reasoning and Logical Thinking (Cross-Cutting Strand)

## 1. Curriculum Architecture & Grade Progression Overview

The National Curriculum of Pakistan (NCP) 2022–23 emphasizes conceptual understanding, mathematical reasoning, the Concrete-Pictorial-Abstract (CPA) pedagogical progression, and real-life problem solving.

| Grade | Primary Curriculum Focus | MathQuest Worlds | Key Domains & Strands |
|:---:|---|---|---|
| **1** | Foundational number sense, place value (1–100), simple operations, basic 2D shapes, measurement & currency | • Numberland Safari<br>• Shapes & Time Valley | Numbers & Operations, Measurement, Geometry |
| **2** | 3-digit numbers, place value, operations with regrouping, multiplication as repeated addition, 3D shapes, standard units | • Meadow of Numbers<br>• Dimension Grove | Numbers & Operations, Geometry, Measurement & Data |
| **3** | 4-digit numbers, Roman numerals, fractions, line segments, angles, perimeter, Pakistani currency, bar graphs | • Realm of Numerals<br>• Geometry Gardens | Numbers & Operations, Geometry, Measurement & Data |
| **4** | Numbers to 100,000, factors, multiples, HCF/LCM, fraction operations, decimals, protractor angles, area/perimeter | • Valley of Multiples<br>• Geometric Citadel | Numbers & Operations, Geometry, Measurement & Data |
| **5** | Large numbers (1M), order of operations (BODMAS), fraction multiplication/division, decimals, angles in shapes, volume | • Isle of Operations<br>• Polygon Peak | Numbers & Operations, Geometry, Measurement & Data |
| **6** | Integers, rational numbers, ratios, proportions, basic algebraic expressions, equations, angle types, coordinates | • Integers & Algebra Domain<br>• Coordinate & Plane Coast | Numbers & Operations, Algebra, Geometry, Statistics |
| **7** | Rational arithmetic, laws of exponents, polynomial operations, linear equations, commercial math (profit/loss/zakat), circles | • Rational Realm<br>• Commercial & Circle City | Numbers & Operations, Algebra, Geometry, Statistics |
| **8** | Real numbers, squares/cubes, algebraic identities, quadratic factoring, simultaneous equations, Pythagoras, solid mensuration | • Real Numbers & Algebra Keep<br>• Geometric Fortress | Real Numbers, Algebra, Geometry, Mensuration & Data |
| **9** | Real & complex numbers, logarithms, matrices, determinants, Cramer's rule, polynomial factorization, linear graphs, coordinate geometry | • Matrix & Logarithm Nexus<br>• Cartesian & Deductive Ridge | Number Systems, Matrices, Algebra, Geometry |

## 2. Detailed Grade-by-Grade Curriculum Mapping

### Grade 1

#### World 1: Numberland Safari (`grade=1`, `icon=compass`, `theme_color=emerald`)
* **Strand:** Numbers and Operations
* **Topic 1.1: Counting & Place Value (1–100)**
  * **Curriculum Standard:** NCP-MATH-G1-NO.01
  * **Official Student Learning Outcome (SLO):** Count, read, write, compare, and order whole numbers up to 100; identify place values of Tens and Ones.
  * **Learning Objectives:** Count objects in groups of tens and ones; order numbers from least to greatest; compare numbers using greater than/less than.
  * **Mission 1.1.1: Safari Number Quest**
    - Category: Concept Exploration | Difficulty: Easy | 8 Questions
    - Focus: Counting objects up to 20, ordering numbers, identifying one more/one less.
  * **Mission 1.1.2: The Tens & Ones Outpost**
    - Category: Practice | Difficulty: Medium | 8 Questions
    - Focus: Place value decomposition of 2-digit numbers into tens and ones.

* **Topic 1.2: Basic Addition and Subtraction**
  * **Curriculum Standard:** NCP-MATH-G1-NO.02
  * **Official Student Learning Outcome (SLO):** Add and subtract 1-digit and 2-digit numbers within 20 and up to 100 without regrouping, using visual models and mental strategies.
  * **Learning Objectives:** Combine sets of objects; perform single-digit addition facts; subtract single-digit numbers from teen numbers; solve simple real-world story problems.
  * **Mission 1.2.1: River Crossing Addition**
    - Category: Practice | Difficulty: Easy | 8 Questions
    - Focus: Addition facts within 10 and 20, visual bar models, combining sets.
  * **Mission 1.2.2: Jungle Subtraction Trail**
    - Category: Practice | Difficulty: Medium | 8 Questions
    - Focus: Subtraction within 20, difference between quantities, taking away.

#### World 2: Shapes & Time Valley (`grade=1`, `icon=map`, `theme_color=cyan`)
* **Strand:** Geometry and Measurement
* **Topic 2.1: 2D Shapes & Spatial Sense**
  * **Curriculum Standard:** NCP-MATH-G1-GM.01
  * **Official Student Learning Outcome (SLO):** Identify, name, and sort basic 2D shapes (circle, square, rectangle, triangle) based on number of sides and corners; use positional language (above, below, inside, outside, left, right).
  * **Learning Objectives:** Recognize shapes in everyday objects; count sides and vertices of 2D shapes; interpret relative spatial positions.
  * **Mission 2.1.1: Shape Detective Expedition**
    - Category: Concept Exploration | Difficulty: Easy | 8 Questions
    - Focus: Attribute sorting of circles, squares, rectangles, triangles, and spatial positions.

* **Topic 2.2: Measurement, Time & Currency**
  * **Curriculum Standard:** NCP-MATH-G1-GM.02
  * **Official Student Learning Outcome (SLO):** Compare lengths, heights, and masses using non-standard and comparative units; read clock times on the hour (o'clock); identify Pakistani currency coins (Rs. 1, 2, 5, 10) and notes (Rs. 10, 20, 50, 100).
  * **Learning Objectives:** Compare heavier/lighter, taller/shorter; tell time to the hour on an analog clock; calculate simple coin combinations.
  * **Mission 2.2.1: Time & Coin Bazaar**
    - Category: Practice | Difficulty: Medium | 8 Questions
    - Focus: O'clock analog clock reading, non-standard length comparison, basic Pakistani rupee values.

### Grade 2

#### World 1: Meadow of Numbers (`grade=2`, `icon=sparkles`, `theme_color=teal`)
* **Strand:** Numbers and Operations
* **Topic 1.1: 3-Digit Numbers & Place Value (Up to 1000)**
  * **Curriculum Standard:** NCP-MATH-G2-NO.01
  * **Official Student Learning Outcome (SLO):** Read, write, compare, and order numbers up to 1000; decompose numbers into hundreds, tens, and ones.
  * **Learning Objectives:** Expanded notation of 3-digit numbers; skip-counting by 2s, 5s, 10s, and 100s; ordering numbers.
  * **Mission 1.1.1: Triple-Digit Meadow**
    - Category: Concept Exploration | Difficulty: Easy | 8 Questions
    - Focus: Place value of digits in 3-digit numbers, expanded form ($345 = 300 + 40 + 5$), number comparisons.

  * **Curriculum Standard:** NCP-MATH-G2-NO.02
  * **Official Student Learning Outcome (SLO):** Add and subtract up to 3-digit numbers with and without regrouping (carrying/borrowing); recognize multiplication as repeated addition of equal groups.
  * **Learning Objectives:** Two-digit addition with carrying; two-digit subtraction with borrowing; model equal groups as multiplication arrays.
  * **Mission 1.2.1: Regrouping Rapids**
    - Category: Practice | Difficulty: Medium | 8 Questions
    - Focus: 2-digit and 3-digit addition/subtraction with regrouping, word problems.
  * **Mission 1.2.2: Repeated Addition Orchard**
    - Category: Practice | Difficulty: Medium | 8 Questions
    - Focus: Modeling multiplication using equal groups ($4 \times 3 = 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 12$) and arrays.

#### World 2: Dimension Grove (`grade=2`, `icon=globe`, `theme_color=blue`)

  * **Curriculum Standard:** NCP-MATH-G2-GM.01

  * **Learning Objectives:** Distinguish flat and curved faces; count vertices and edges of 3D solids; identify rule of visual and numerical patterns.
  * **Mission 2.1.1: Solid Geometry Sanctuary**
    - Category: Concept Exploration | Difficulty: Easy | 8 Questions
    - Focus: Identification of solids, counting faces/edges, repeating pattern rules.

* **Topic 2.2: Standard Units, Clock (Half/Quarter Hour) & Pictographs**
  * **Curriculum Standard:** NCP-MATH-G2-GM.02
  * **Official Student Learning Outcome (SLO):** Measure length in meters and centimeters, mass in kilograms and grams, volume in liters; read time to the half-hour and quarter-hour; interpret simple pictographs and tally marks.
  * **Learning Objectives:** Choose appropriate metric units; read analog clock to half-past and quarter-past; extract values from pictographs.
  * **Mission 2.2.1: The Metric Clocktower**
    - Category: Practice | Difficulty: Medium | 8 Questions
    - Focus: Measuring with cm/m, half-hour and quarter-hour time reading, tally charts and pictographs.

## 3. Pedagogical Quality and Question Standards

Every question generated across Grades 1–10 adheres to the following rigorous criteria:
1. **Mathematical Accuracy:** Every problem statement is verified for correctness, clear terminology, and single unambiguous correct answers.
2. **Step-by-Step Solutions:** Every question provides an explicit, pedagogical step-by-step derivation suitable for student self-study and teacher previews.
3. **Common Misconceptions (Diagnostic Feedback):** Real student errors (e.g., adding numerators and denominators, sign errors in negative numbers, forgetting to divide after subtraction) are mapped to targeted diagnostic feedback.
4. **Question Types Supported:** Strictly uses existing supported types:
   - Multiple Choice (`multiple_choice`) with 4 distinct options e.g. `["A) 1", "B) 2", "C) 3", "D) 4"]`.
   - Numeric Entry (`numeric`) with exact numerical values or integer/fraction answers.
5. **Difficulty Progression:** Questions within each mission cover:
   - **Easy:** Basic concept identification and direct calculation.
   - **Medium:** Multi-step calculation, application, and word problems.
   - **Hard / Boss:** Multi-concept synthesis, higher-order reasoning, and algebraic modeling.
6. **Gamification & XP Integrity:** Follows the established MathQuest reward engine: 10 XP per correct question attempt, 20 XP mission completion bonus, and passing accuracy threshold of 60%.
